Common Questions

What is the cost?
The State of WV sets the notary fee at $10 per stamp. We add a separate travel fee based on your location to cover time and mileage.
Can you verify I-9 forms?
Yes! We act as an Authorized Representative for your employer to verify identity documents and complete Form I-9.
Where can we meet?
We can come to your home, office, hospital, nursing home, or meet at a public place like a coffee shop or library.
What do I need to bring?
You must have a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, Passport, etc.). All signers must be present.
