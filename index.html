FAQ

How do I pay?
We accept Cash (USD) upon arrival. Deposits via EzeePayments.
Where do we meet?
Look for the P&T Island Tours sign with YOUR NAME at the airport exit.
Is the price per person?
Private transfers are typically for the vehicle (1-4 passengers). Larger groups have a small per-person fee.
Do you provide car seats?
Yes! Car seats are available upon request for a small fee. Just let us know in the booking form.
Can we stop for food?
Absolutely! Since it's a private tour, we can stop for jerk chicken, patties, or groceries on the way.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations made more than 24 hours in advance receive a full refund of the deposit.

Terms & Conditions

1. Booking & Deposits: A 20-30% deposit is required to secure all bookings. The remaining balance is payable in Cash (USD) or via credit card upon arrival.

2. Cancellations: Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time are eligible for a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable.

3. Flight Delays: We actively monitor all incoming flights. If your flight is delayed, we will wait for you at no extra cost. For hotel pickups, a 15-minute grace period applies.

4. Liability: P&T Island Tours is a JTB-certified and insured operator. However, we are not responsible for lost personal items left in our vehicles. Passengers are responsible for their belongings.

5. Passenger Conduct: We reserve the right to refuse service to any passenger who poses a threat to the safety of our driver or the vehicle. No refunds will be given in such cases.
