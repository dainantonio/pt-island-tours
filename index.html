Terms of Use

1. Booking & Deposits

All bookings require a 20-30% non-refundable deposit to secure your vehicle and driver for the requested date. This deposit is processed via our secure EzeePayments link. The remaining balance is due upon arrival and can be paid in Cash (USD) or via Credit Card (subject to a small processing fee).

2. Cancellations & Refunds

We understand that travel plans change. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time are eligible for a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made within 24 hours of the pickup time are non-refundable.

3. Flight Delays

We actively monitor incoming flights using the flight number you provide. If your flight is delayed, there is no need to worry. We will adjust our schedule and be there waiting for you when you land, at no extra cost. For hotel pickups, we offer a 15-minute grace period before the driver attempts to contact you.

4. Liability & Insurance

P&T Island Tours is a JTB-certified and fully insured operator. We prioritize your safety above all else. However, we are not responsible for any lost, stolen, or damaged personal items left in our vehicles. Passengers are responsible for ensuring they have all their belongings before exiting the vehicle.

5. Passenger Conduct

We reserve the right to refuse service to any passenger who poses a threat to the safety of our driver or the vehicle. Consumption of illegal substances or excessive alcohol that impairs judgment or safety is strictly prohibited. In such cases, the service will be terminated immediately without refund.

6. Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Any personal information collected (Name, Phone, Flight Details) is used solely for the purpose of facilitating your transfer or tour. We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except as required for payment processing.
