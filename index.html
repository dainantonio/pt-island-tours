Common Questions

How do I pay?

We accept Cash (USD) upon arrival. For deposits, we accept EzeePayments (Visa/Mastercard). Details provided after booking confirmation.

Where do I meet the driver?

At the airport, our driver will be holding a P&T Island Tours sign with YOUR NAME at the exit of the Arrivals terminal.

Is the price per person?

Private transfer prices are typically for the vehicle (1-4 passengers). Larger groups have a small per-person fee.

Terms & Conditions

1. Bookings & Deposits: A deposit is required to secure all bookings. The remaining balance is due in cash (USD) or card upon arrival.

2. Cancellations: Cancellations made more than 24 hours in advance will receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 24 hours are non-refundable.

3. Waiting Policy: We monitor all flights. We will wait for delayed flights at no extra cost. For hotel pickups, we offer a 15-minute grace period.

4. Liability: P&T Island Tours is fully insured. However, we are not responsible for lost personal items left in vehicles.

5. Conduct: We reserve the right to refuse service to any passenger who poses a threat to the safety of our driver or vehicle.
